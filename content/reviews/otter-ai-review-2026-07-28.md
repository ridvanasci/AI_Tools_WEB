## What Is Otter.ai?
Otter.ai is an AI-powered transcription tool designed to automatically generate text from audio and video recordings. It leverages artificial intelligence to provide fast, accurate, and secure transcription services, catering to the needs of individuals, businesses, and organizations. With Otter.ai, users can easily record, transcribe, and share conversations, meetings, and interviews.

## Key Features
* Automatic transcription: Otter.ai uses AI to transcribe audio and video recordings quickly and accurately.
* Real-time transcription: Users can view transcripts in real-time, allowing for immediate review and editing.
* Multi-language support: Otter.ai supports transcription in over 40 languages, making it a versatile tool for global users.

* Integration with popular platforms: Otter.ai integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and other popular video conferencing tools.
