## What Is Otter.ai?
Otter.ai is an AI-powered transcription tool designed to help individuals and businesses convert audio and video recordings into text. It uses automatic speech recognition technology to generate transcripts, making it an ideal solution for freelancers, agencies, and developers who need to transcribe interviews, meetings, and other audio or video content. With its intuitive interface and high accuracy rate, Otter.ai has become a popular choice for those seeking efficient transcription services.

## Key Features
* Automatic transcription: Otter.ai uses AI to transcribe audio and video recordings with high accuracy
* Real-time transcription: Transcripts are generated in real-time, allowing users to follow along with the conversation
* Speaker identification: Otter.ai can identify different speakers and label them accordingly in the transcript
* Customizable vocabulary: Users can add custom words and phrases to improve transcription accuracy
* Integration with other tools: Otter.ai integrates with popular tools like Zoom, Google Meet, and Dropbox
