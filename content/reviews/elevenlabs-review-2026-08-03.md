## What Is ElevenLabs?
ElevenLabs is an AI voice generation tool that allows users to create high-quality, realistic voiceovers for various applications, including videos, audiobooks, and podcasts. It utilizes advanced artificial intelligence algorithms to synthesize natural-sounding voices from text inputs. With its user-friendly interface and extensive voice library, ElevenLabs has become a popular choice among content creators and developers.

## Key Features
* **Voice Library**: ElevenLabs offers a vast library of voices in multiple languages, allowing users to choose the perfect voice for their projects.
* **Customization**: Users can adjust voice parameters such as pitch, tone, and speed to fine-tune the output.
* **Text-to-Speech**: The tool supports text-to-speech functionality, enabling users to generate voiceovers from written text.
* **Audio Editing**: ElevenLabs provides basic audio editing features, including trim, split, and merge functions.
* **Integration**: The platform offers APIs and SDKs for seamless integration with other applications and services.
