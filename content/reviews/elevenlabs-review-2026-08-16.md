## What Is ElevenLabs?
ElevenLabs is an AI-powered voice generation tool that utilizes advanced speech synthesis technologies to create realistic and high-quality voiceovers. It allows users to generate audio files from text inputs, making it a valuable resource for various applications such as video production, podcasting, and e-learning. With its robust features and user-friendly interface, ElevenLabs has gained popularity among professionals and creators.
